\begin{abstract}
We state the skeleton theorem for an integral matrix $E$ with determinant
one and an arbitrary positive rational diagonal matrix $\widetilde D$ such
that $K=E\widetilde D$ is skew-symmetric. The Lean formalization constructs
the real smooth Liouville geometry and proves the full dynamical skeleton
identity for all these inputs. The accompanying blueprint gives the
weighted geometric argument and explains the scope of the formal result.
\end{abstract}

\section{Input data and exchange space}
Let $n\ge0$, let $M\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\}$ be mutable, and let $F$ be
its complement. Fix
\[
 E\in M_n(\Z),\quad \det E=1,\qquad
 \widetilde D=\operatorname{diag}(d_1,\ldots,d_n),\quad
 d_j\in\mathbb Q_{>0},\qquad
 K=E\widetilde D,\quad K^{\mathsf T}=-K.
\]
Thus $E_{ij}d_j=-E_{ji}d_i$, rather than $E_{ij}=-E_{ji}$.
Require the mutable block $B=E_{M,M}$ to be acyclic: the arrows
$i\to j$ with $B_{ij}>0$ have no directed cycle. No acyclicity is
required on the full set of indices. The unimodular completion is part of
the input; existence of such a completion for every exchange matrix is
not asserted. Equivalently one may require $E\in GL_n(\Z)$: a nonsingular real skew-symmetric matrix
has positive determinant, so $\det E=\det K/\det\widetilde D>0$,
while unimodularity gives $\det E=\pm1$.

Use \emph{column} exchange exponents throughout:
\[
 P_i^\pm(z)=\prod_{j=1}^n z_j^{[\pm E_{ji}]_+}.
\]
\[
 X_E=\left\{(z,z')\in\C^n\times\C^M:
 \begin{array}{ll}
 z_i z_i'=P_i^+(z)+P_i^-(z)&(i\in M),\\
 z_f\ne0 &(f\in F)
 \end{array}\right\}.
\]
Here $[a]_+=\max(a,0)$. Give $X_E$ the induced topology. Frozen
coordinates remain variable elements of $\C^*$; no principal-coefficient
assumption is made. The initial torus is
$T=\{z_1\cdots z_n\ne0\}\subset X_E$. On it set
\[
 c_i=Ee_i,\qquad t_i=z^{c_i}=P_i^+/P_i^-,\qquad
 \Omega_T=\frac12\sum_{j,l}K_{jl}\,\dd\log z_j\wedge\dd\log z_l.
\]
The integer exponents come from $E$, not from the generally rational $K$.

\section{Independently defined subsets}
Define the coordinate cap without reference to any flow:
\[
 C_E=\{(z,z')\in X_E: |z_f|=1\ (f\in F),\quad
 |z_i|\le1,\ (1-|z_i|^2)z_i'=0\ (i\in M)\}.
\]
Call $I\subseteq M$ independent if $E_{ij}=0$ for all $i,j\in I$, and put
\[
 \Lambda_I=\{(z,z')\in X_E:
 z_i'=0,\ |z_i|\le1\ (i\in I),\quad |z_j|=1\ (j\notin I)\}.
\]
For a real flow $\Phi$ on $X_E$, independently define
\[
 \Skel(\Phi)=\{p\in X_E:
 \overline{\{\Phi_t(p):t\ge0\}}^{\,X_E}\text{ is compact}\}.
\]
The closure is taken in the actual exchange space.

\section{The weighted geometric theorem}
\begin{theorem}[Skew-symmetrizable skeleton theorem]\label{thm:target}
For every $(n,E,\widetilde D,M)$ satisfying the hypotheses above, the
fixed space $X_E$ admits a smooth real manifold structure of dimension
$2n$, a smooth real one-form $\lambda$, a smooth vector field $Z$, and
a jointly smooth flow $\Phi:\R\times X_E\to X_E$ such that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item The actual coordinate inclusion in $\C^n\times\C^M$ is a smooth
immersion, and $T$ is dense.
\item $\omega=\dd\lambda$ is nondegenerate and
$\omega|_T=\im\Omega_T$.
\item $\iota_Z\omega=\lambda$, $\Phi_0(p)=p$,
$\Phi_{t+s}(p)=\Phi_t(\Phi_s(p))$, and
$\frac{\dd}{\dd t}\Phi_t(p)=Z(\Phi_t(p))$ for every real $s,t$.
Thus this same field is complete in both time directions.
\item For this same flow,
\[
 \boxed{\Skel(\Phi)=C_E=
 \bigcup_{I\subseteq M\text{ independent}}\Lambda_I.}
\]
Each $\Lambda_I$ in this union is compact.
\end{enumerate}
For independent $I$, the node chart
$U_I=\{z_j\ne0\ (j\notin I)\}$ has the form
$\mathcal N^{I}\times(\C^*)^{n-2|I|}$, where
$\mathcal N=\{(x,y,t):xy=1+t,\ t\ne0\}$; in particular $2|I|\le n$.
\end{theorem}
The quantifier order is $\forall(n,E,\widetilde D,M)\ \exists
(\text{smooth structure},\lambda,Z,\Phi)$. No flow, primitive,
completeness, nondegeneracy, or skeleton inclusion is supplied as an
additional hypothesis. The real smooth theorem is proved in Lean;
the blueprint also explains the local extension of $\Omega_T$.

\section{Why the weights cannot be discarded}
Write $z_j=e^{u_j+\mathrm i\theta_j}$, $v_i=c_i(u)$ and
$\eta_i=c_i(\theta)$. The blueprint constructs
\[
 \lambda_{\rm tor}=u^{\mathsf T}K\,\dd\theta,\qquad
 H=\sum_{i\in M}d_i h(u_i)\chi_i\rho(v_i)\beta(\eta_i),\qquad
 \lambda=\lambda_{\rm tor}+\dd H.
\]
The identities $Ke_i=d_ic_i$ and
$c_i^{\mathsf T}K^{-1}=-e_i^{\mathsf T}/d_i$ give
\[
 \dot u_i=u_i-h(u_i)\chi_i\rho(v_i)\beta'(\eta_i),\qquad
 \dot\eta_i=d_i^{-1}\partial_{u_i}H.
\]
The radial equations have the same form as in the unweighted case
because the weights cancel, not because $d_i$ is assumed to be one.
Every node symplectic block and its primitive carries the factor $d_i$.
Positivity of $d_i$ and invertibility of $K$ enter the construction.

A concrete non-skew input covered by the universal theorem is
\[
 E=\begin{pmatrix}
 0&1&-1&0\\-2&0&0&-1\\1&0&0&0\\0&1&0&0
 \end{pmatrix},\qquad
 \widetilde D=\operatorname{diag}(1,2,1,2),\qquad M=\{1,2\}.
\]
Here $\det E=1$, $E\widetilde D$ is skew-symmetric, and the mutable
quiver is acyclic, but $E_{12}=1\ne2=-E_{21}$. This example checks
that the input is genuinely broader; it does not replace the theorem
quantified over all admissible inputs.

\section{Lean correspondence and verification}
\lean{Seed} contains the integral $E$, positive rational weights $d_i$,
weighted skew-symmetry, mutable indices and mutable acyclicity.
\lean{Seed.Unimodular} means $\det E=1$.
\lean{FullSkeleton.firstGeometricTarget} proves the universally quantified
existence theorem. The exchange monomials use column exponents
definitionally, and the torus form uses $K=E\widetilde D$ with the sign
and factor $1/2$ displayed above.

The library proves both the cap and stratified skeleton identities,
constructs the node charts and smooth structure, and proves the chart
rank and dimension bound.
\lean{PdfTargetConsumer.pdf_target}, in
\lean{verification/FullSkeletonConsumer.lean}, assembles the published
clauses into one checked statement. The repository's proof-to-code map
lists the individual declarations.

The $\widetilde D=I$ specialization is recovered by
\lean{SkewSeed.skeleton_recovery}. On the old exchange-point carrier it
gives the original torus and cap descriptions and the skeleton of the
conjugate flow at the same times. The identification of carriers is the
identity on ambient coordinates; the logarithmic form uses the old
matrix $B$ with no sign change. Smooth witnesses are existential on the
weighted reading of that space. No term identity with the former
library's particular atlas, primitive or flow is asserted.

The displayed non-skew example and its side conditions are checked in
\lean{Examples}; its geometry is an instance of the universal theorem.

\section{Scope}
The formal input uses $\det E=1$ explicitly. The elementary equivalence
with an integral unimodular completion under the weighted skewness
hypothesis is explained in Section~1; the determinant-positivity argument
itself is not a separate theorem in this library.
The result concerns real smooth Liouville geometry. It does not claim a
separately formalized holomorphic extension of $\Omega_T$, singular
completions, arbitrary cyclic seeds, cap-product descriptions, generalized
cocores, or categorical mirror symmetry. Frozen coordinates remain
variable and no principal-coefficient restriction is imposed.
